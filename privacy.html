Privacy

What Google tells us

When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile. We use your email to check that your account is allowed in. We do not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or anything else.

What we save

Your habits, campaigns, stories, and settings are saved on our server under your account ID so they sync between devices. A copy is also kept in your browser, along with your sign-in session, which lasts 30 days.

How the AI uses your content

When you press "Tell the next beat", we send your hero, genre, notes, habit names, and recent story text to Anthropic's Claude API to write the next part of your story. Nothing else from your account is sent.

Deleting your data

Signing out clears the copy in your browser. You can export or delete past campaigns on the Past page. To delete everything saved on the server, contact cgblack5@byu.edu.
