What signing in does

  • There is no password to make. Google confirms it is you.
  • Google shares only your email address and basic profile with this app.
  • The app cannot read your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or anything else in your account.
  • Your habits and stories are saved to your account, so they follow you to any device.
